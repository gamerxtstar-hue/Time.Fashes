Chapter I · 1927

The founding.

Étienne Fashes was 24 when he opened the workshop. He believed a watchmaker's greatest tool was patience, and that any timepiece produced in fewer than forty days was not yet finished.

The rule remains. So does the bench.

Chapter II · 1963

The Caliber F27.

Our in-house movement was designed over eight years, prototyped forty-two times, and finally released to nineteen loyal collectors. Every F27 today still traces its geometry to the same drawings.

28,800 vibrations per hour. Five days of reserve. Zero apologies.

Chapter III · Today

A quiet continuation.

Fashes remains independent. No board, no shareholders, no product-cycle pressure. Just four master watchmakers, one atelier, and the same rule we have kept for ninety-nine years:

\"If it is not right, it is not ready.\"
